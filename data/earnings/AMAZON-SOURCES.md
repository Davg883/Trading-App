## Gross versus net purchases discrepancy

The user brief describes an article calling approximately USD 77.3 billion of quarterly purchases **net**. No article URL or full article was supplied, so that attribution remains an **unverified user-reported article claim**, not a verified media source.

The official Q3 cash-flow statement distinguishes the following rows:

| Cash measure | Q3 2025 | Q3 2026 | TTM to September 2025 | TTM to September 2026 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Gross purchases of property and equipment, expressed as positive cash outflow magnitude | 35095 | 77259 | 120131 | 215192 |
| Proceeds from sales and incentives | 867 | 1381 | 4228 | 4535 |
| Net purchases, gross outflow less proceeds | 34228 | 75878 | 115903 | 210657 |
| Operating cash flow | 35525 | 42422 | 130691 | 168300 |

Thus the approximately USD 77.3 billion number corresponds to **gross** purchases of 77,259 million. Quarterly **net** purchases are 75,878 million after 1,381 million of proceeds. TTM net purchases are a separate 210,657 million. Do not substitute the quarterly gross number for quarterly net capex or use either as a TTM measure. Preserve the statement cash-flow signs alongside any positive-outflow convention used for ratios.
