The supplied case study was checked against the SEC-hosted company earnings release, rather than treating the pasted commentary as a verified financial dataset.

- Primary release: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000080/googexhibit991q32026.htm
- Acceptance record: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000080/0001652044-26-000080-index.htm
- Revenue-estimate discrepancy: https://apnews.com/article/1acf6aff00d4dc084c5243d613307388 and https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/why-is-alphabet-stock-plunging-afterhours-today-93CH-4981395

The JSON contains a manually reviewed factual extraction, not an automated live SEC feed. Company-reported figures are unaudited. `verified` means matched to the primary document, not independently audited economic truth.

The SEC index reports acceptance at 16:01:33 Eastern on 28 October. Its UTC equivalent, 20:01:33Z, is used as the source's known public filing timestamp. It is not claimed to be the exact first distribution time of the press release. Retrieval occurred on 29 October at approximately 17:15:23Z and is preserved as receipt time. Prior-year comparison figures were learned from this same release and retain this receipt time; they are not backdated.

The quarterly cash-flow bridge is calculated from the three-month columns, not year-to-date cash flows. Free cash flow is the company's non-GAAP OCF-less-capex measure. Segment operating margin is not a fully allocated infrastructure return. Capital expenditure is stored as positive spending, with subtraction performed by the bridge.

Media estimates of revenue differ (128.2 versus 127.97 billion USD). The original vendor collection times were not available, and the FCF estimate's accounting basis is not established. These observations are retained separately and blocked from scored surprise calculations. Missing metadata is never filled with a guessed timestamp.

No price quote, share-price return, model result, forward forecast or trade has been inferred from a headline. Market reaction can be studied only after a registered price window and sourced observations are supplied. The original Sky article URL and original publication timestamp were not included in the user attachment; the article intake panel supports adding them without inventing them.
